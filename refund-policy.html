Consumer Protection Commitment

Refund & Cancellation Policy

UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 & Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 Compliant

14-Day Statutory Money-Back Guarantee

At Shoppaza (SAJID SERVICES LTD), we are committed to complete customer satisfaction. If you experience technical incompatibility or are unsatisfied with your digital software product, you are entitled to a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase.

1. Scope of the Policy

This Refund Policy applies to all direct digital software licenses, downloadable tools, and cloud automation scripts purchased through shoppaza.com, operated by SAJID SERVICES LTD (Company Registration No. SC874640, Edinburgh, Scotland).

2. 14-Day Cooling-Off Period & Eligibility

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers purchasing digital goods retain the statutory right to cancel their purchase within 14 calendar days of transaction completion.

To qualify for a prompt refund:

  • The refund request must be lodged within fourteen (14) days from the initial payment timestamp.
  • You must provide your order reference number, invoice number, or purchase email address.
  • For defect or technical malfunction claims, our engineering team will offer immediate bug rectification or license credit if preferred by the customer.

3. Step-by-Step Refund Application Process

Requesting a refund is frictionless. Please follow these straightforward steps:

  1. Transmit an email to our official billing desk at support@shoppaza.com (or via our Contact Form).
  2. Set the subject line to: "Refund Request - [Your Order / Transaction ID]".
  3. Briefly state the reason for cancellation (e.g., technical incompatibility, accidental purchase, or requirement change).
  4. Our Edinburgh billing department will review and confirm receipt of your request within twenty-four (24) business hours.

4. Method of Refund & Payout Timeframes

Once your refund request is authorized by our team:

  • The refund is executed immediately through our merchant payment gateway (Stripe).
  • 100% of the purchase amount is credited directly back to the original funding payment method (credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay).
  • Depending on your card issuer or banking institution, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 10 business days.
  • No administrative restocking fees or penalty charges are deducted from eligible refunds.

5. License Key Deactivation

Upon issuance of a full refund, the cryptographic software license key associated with your order will be deactivated in our central licensing server, and technical support access for that specific order will cease.
