2. Categories of Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to fulfill software orders and comply with statutory UK fiscal reporting:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, company affiliation, billing address, and email address used for digital license key transmission.
  • Transaction Records: Software license tier purchased, timestamp, transaction ID, and VAT receipt data.
  • Payment Card Data: We do NOT collect or store full credit/debit card numbers. All card transactions are tokenized and processed securely by Stripe Inc. in compliance with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, browser type, and anonymous telemetry strictly utilized for fraud mitigation and server load optimization.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases recognized under UK law:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing necessary to deliver your digital software download and license key.
  • Legal Obligation: Maintaining accounting and tax records required by HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing unauthorized payment chargebacks, fraud, and system security.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only share information with vetted infrastructure processors essential for order completion:

  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd: For payment gateway processing, anti-fraud evaluation, and card tokenization.
  • Cloudflare Inc: For edge network caching, DDOS mitigation, and secure content delivery.

5. Data Retention Guidelines

Personal information associated with software orders is retained for a period of six (6) years following the transaction to satisfy UK statutory accounting requirements (Companies Act 2006 / HMRC compliance).

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a data subject, you hold statutory rights including:

  • The right to access the personal records we hold about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") where no overriding statutory obligation persists.
  • The right to lodge a formal complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Privacy Officer via support@shoppaza.com.
