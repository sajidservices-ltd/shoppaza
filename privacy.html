1. Data Controller Identification

This Privacy Notice explains how SAJID SERVICES LTD ("Shoppaza", "we", "us", or "our") collects, protects, and uses customer personal information when you browse or order physical goods on shoppaza.com. SAJID SERVICES LTD operates as the registered Data Controller under UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact Details: Name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone number (used strictly for order fulfillment and delivery tracking).
  • Payment Transaction Data: Payment method choice, transaction IDs, and currency preference. (Note: Complete credit card numbers are tokenized directly by Stripe and never touch Shoppaza web servers).
  • Technical Telemetry: IP address, browser type, and cookie identifiers for session management and anti-fraud velocity checks.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data under Article 6 of the UK GDPR primarily based on Contractual Necessity (to deliver purchased products to your doorstep) and Legal Compliance (maintaining accounting records for UK HMRC).

4. Third-Party Disclosures (Stripe & Couriers)

We never sell or rent your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with necessary operational partners:

  • Payment Processor (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd): For secure PCI-compliant card tokenization.
  • Shipping Couriers (Royal Mail, DHL Express, FedEx): Your name, delivery address, and phone number are transmitted solely for package transit and delivery notification.

5. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"). You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting admin@shoppaza.com.
